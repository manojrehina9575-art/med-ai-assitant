Clinical Workflow

Draft, Review, Sign.

The workflow radiologists and pathologists already trust, with the initial heavy lifting prepared for them. Strict protocol governance enforced by code.

01

Study Ingestion & Extraction

Medical imaging (DICOM, X-Ray, CT, MRI) or lab panels arrive from your PACS/LIS. The neural engine extracts biomarker matrices, visual abnormalities, and prepares a structured clinical draft.

02

Clinician Reading Worklist

Drafts queue on the reading worklist prioritized by urgency triage. Reviewing physicians accept, edit, or reject findings with full clinical autonomy. Critical alerts trigger instant escalation protocols.

03

Cryptographic Sign-Off

Signing permanently attributes the report to the licensed practitioner with an immutable timestamp and audit trail. The finalized report syncs downstream via FHIR R4.

Enterprise Infrastructure

Built for the parts nobody demos.

Clinical AI cannot survive in production on models alone. It requires database isolation, auditability, consent frameworks, and regulatory interoperability.

PostgreSQL Row-Level Security

Tenant isolation is enforced in the database engine itself. A missed filter in application code cannot leak patient data across hospital boundaries.

Immutable 6-Year Audit Trail

Every report generation, clinician edit, sign-off, and view action is written to an append-only audit ledger with 6-year retention compliance.

FHIR R4 & ABDM Standard

Native FHIR R4 resources (DiagnosticReport, Observation, ImagingStudy) ready for seamless integration with ABDM (Ayushman Bharat) and hospital EHRs.

ICD-10 & LOINC Validation

Conclusions and lab parameters are rigorously standardized against verified medical ontologies. Unmatched terms remain explicit text rather than hallucinated codes.

Prescription Safety Checks

Automated cross-reactivity, duplicate therapy, and contraindication screening with daily dosage caps. Critical alerts block issuance until clinician acknowledgement.

Consent & PHI Redaction

Granular per-patient consent tracking for research use, automated client-side PHI de-identification, and data residency in sovereign Indian data centers.
